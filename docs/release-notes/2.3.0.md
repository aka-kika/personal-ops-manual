Notarized Developer ID build. Unzip and drop into /Applications. macOS 26 or newer.

New in 2.3.0
- **Drift:** a new sidebar row that checks what pages claim against this Mac: installed app versions and notarization, MCP servers in ~/.claude.json, listening ports, launch jobs and CLI versions. It lists the pages that no longer match, newest first, with a score and what drags it down.
- **Report from Drift:** one click adds a line to the Inbox for the operator, in the same shape as report_change. Drift never edits a page.
- **checks: in the frontmatter:** a page lists its checkable facts, one per line (`port 4319 node`, `app "Personal Ops Manual" 2.3.0 notarized`). Saving a page in the app keeps the list.
- **Inbox in the app:** the reports readers filed open inside the app, newest first, with a jump to their page, instead of in another editor.
- **How often Drift checks:** Settings > General > Drift, once a day by default; opening Drift and Check now check right away.
- **Parked:** pages with status parked or archived leave the category lists and counts and sit under one quiet Parked row at the bottom of the sidebar. Search still finds them, and Drift skips them.
- **What's new:** this sheet, once after each update and from the Help menu.

Changes in 2.3.0
- **TestFlight:** the new category has its own icon.
- **Inbox:** its sidebar row now opens the reports in the app; Show in Finder is in the list's header.
