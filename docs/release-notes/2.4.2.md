Notarized Developer ID build. Unzip and drop into /Applications. macOS 26 or newer.

New in 2.4.2
- **GitHub and MCP lists look like every list:** one A to Z list with the name, the date and a two-line summary, like Domains. No groups: the small lock or globe before a GitHub name tells private from public, the small person mark shows your own MCP servers, and the quiet dot after a name still means work not saved.
- **One rescan for Drift, in the top-right corner of the window.** Also Cmd-R while Drift is open, and Check Now when you right-click Drift in the sidebar. The list column and the Drift page have no buttons of their own any more; right-click a page in Drift to open it.

Fixes in 2.4.2
- **No footer in the list column:** the "Overview" and "Refreshed" lines at the bottom are gone everywhere. A category's overview page opens from a right-click on "What lives here" in the category pane.
- **Plain names:** pages are no longer renamed on the fly; the GitHub pages themselves now carry their plain names.
