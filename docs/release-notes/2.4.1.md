Notarized Developer ID build. Unzip and drop into /Applications. macOS 26 or newer.

New in 2.4.1
- **Category overview:** pick a category with no page and the right pane shows what lives there (from its "<Category> overview" page), one quiet status line, and the three newest changes. Machines: who is online on Tailscale. GitHub: work not saved, open pull requests, failing builds. MCP: what mcpock says. Accounts: renewals in the next 30 days. Domains: expiry dates. Automations: launch jobs loaded or not, and failed last runs. Services: up or down. Numbers stay quiet; only one that needs care is peach.
- **Overview pages:** a category's overview page sits at the bottom of its list instead of among the pages.

Fixes in 2.4.1
- **GitHub pages show their full text again:** the repo card (branch, uncommitted, tag, builds, folder) sits on top, and the page itself follows below, as before.
- **Every GitHub page is in the list:** pages that are not a repo (the index, Homebrew taps) are under Other pages, and the list says how many pages and how many repos.
- **No corner button:** the button at the top right changed its icon after a click; it is gone. Open in Default App, Copy Path and Refresh are in the right-click menu.
- **GitHub answers reliably:** the repos are fetched 25 at a time (about 3 seconds each) instead of all at once, which often ran into GitHub's time limit (HTTP 502). A passing server error is tried once more.
- **Versions:** "1.0" and "v1.0.0" count as the same version, so they no longer show as out of date.
